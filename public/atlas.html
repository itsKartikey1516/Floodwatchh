From signal to action

How early detection supports readiness

Preparedness systems combine observations, forecasts, and clear local communication. Each signal is useful, but none replaces official warnings.

01

Watch the rain

Rain gauges and forecasts show how much rain is falling, where it is concentrated, and whether catchments are receiving sustained rainfall.

02

Track rising rivers

Upstream river gauges can reveal rising water levels before flows reach downstream communities. Local thresholds help interpret change.

03

Share official warnings

Agencies combine measurements and forecasts to communicate risk. Follow official instructions and act early when local authorities advise.

During an active flood warning

Follow IMD and local emergency authority updates. If told to evacuate, leave promptly. Never walk, swim, or drive through floodwater.

Data & method. Historical exposure figures are from the supplied Flood Affected Area Atlas of India (1998–2022), NRSC / ISRO and NDMA (March 2023).
