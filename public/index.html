When does a reservoir deserve attention?

A fresh level within 5 ft of its published full level triggers an app watch; at or above full/maximum level, it asks you to review official release advisories. These are screening thresholds. Storage or rainfall alone cannot predict structural failure, confirm overtopping, or identify downstream inundation.

No dam-risk verdict until a dated source is available.

Flood-watch levels combine rainfall forecasts and historical mapped flood exposure. They are screening signals, not a measured flood probability or an evacuation order. District map points are approximate. Check official IMD and local authority guidance.

Browser alerts work while this page is running. Closed-app push alerts, physical river sensors and official flood-warning delivery are not connected. The BBMB reservoir table is a dated publication, not a continuous dam-safety sensor feed.
